# Mari's Portfolio

A technical writer's portfolio that showcases documentation craft through process-heavy case studies.

## Language

**Showcase**:
A single featured piece of documentation work, presented as a case study with three layers: Artifact, Process, and Impact. Weighted toward Process.
_Avoid_: Project, case study, portfolio piece, work sample

**Artifact**:
The redacted documentation output itself (KB article excerpt, SOP section, workflow diagram) displayed within a Showcase for reference.
_Avoid_: Sample, document, deliverable

**Process**:
The narrative of decisions Mari made while creating a piece of documentation: why this structure, why this language, what constraints shaped the piece. The primary content of each Showcase.
_Avoid_: Methodology, approach, how-it-was-done

**Impact**:
A concrete statement of what the documented Artifact accomplished for the client or organization.
_Avoid_: Results, outcomes, metrics

**Timeline**:
The condensed career arc displayed in the About section. Reframed as "how I got here," not a work history.
_Avoid_: Resume, CV, work experience, career history

**Landing**:
The entry point of the portfolio. Mari in one sentence, a photo, and immediate access to Showcases.
_Avoid_: Home page, hero section, splash page

## Relationships

- A **Showcase** contains exactly one **Artifact**, one **Process** narrative, and one **Impact** statement
- The **Landing** surfaces all available **Showcases**
- The **Timeline** provides context for the arc between **Showcases** but is not the primary content

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Should the **Showcase** link to the full **Artifact** as a PDF?"
> **Domain expert:** "No. The **Artifact** is an inline excerpt within the **Showcase**. The **Process** narrative is what people come here to read, not the document itself."

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Portfolio" refers to the entire site, not an individual piece. Individual pieces are **Showcases**.
- "Case study" was considered and rejected. It carries corporate consulting connotations that conflict with the brand personality (grounded, generous, warm). **Showcase** is the canonical term.
