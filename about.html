How I got here

I spent 11 years in community development before I became a technical writer. That's not a career change; it's a continuation. In community work, I learned that the difference between a program that lasts and one that fades is whether anyone wrote down how it works. The knowledge was always there. The documentation wasn't.

Technical writing, for me, is the same work in a different context. I take what people know and make it findable, usable, and durable. Whether that's a healthcare SOP, a product support article, or an onboarding workflow, the goal is the same: make sure the right information reaches the right person at the right time.

I care about information architecture because structure is how knowledge becomes accessible. I care about language because clarity is how knowledge becomes usable. And I care about process because good documentation is never a one-time event; it's a living practice.

From community organizing to documentation systems. The thread is knowledge transfer.

  4. 2021 — 2023
